<h2 align="center">🔄 The Old Way vs This Way</h2>

<div align="center">

| 😩 The old way | ✅ With Ledgerline CRM |
|:---|:---|
| 😩 Deals in one sheet, invoices in another | ✅ Pipeline and receivables on one dashboard |
| 😩 No idea what needs action today | ✅ A "to do today" panel: follow-ups due, invoices 31+ days late, deals with no next step |
| 😩 Everyone can edit everything | ✅ Roles from Owner and Admin down to Sales Rep, Accounts and Viewer |
| 😩 Who changed what? | ✅ An activity log of every edit and export |
| 😩 Chasing payments from memory | ✅ A "who to chase" list with the next step for each balance |

</div>

<h2 align="center">✨ What You Get</h2>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🧭 Business Pulse
Five panels: what happened, what is in progress, what is pending, what to do today and what is ahead in the next 30 days.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🗂️ Pipeline Board
Lead, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, Won and Lost columns. Drag a card to another column or use its menu.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 💰 Receivables Ageing
Outstanding balance by days late, invoiced against collected for six months, and a record-payment action.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 📋 Records
All deals and invoices in one table that you can import from Excel or CSV and update.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🔐 Team & Access
Invite people and control what each role can see, edit or export.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🧾 Activity Log
Every change, who made it and when, so the numbers can be trusted.

</td>
</tr>
